### Interactive Music Creation with Pretrained Music Transformer ⭐️ 7.0/10

A user has demonstrated an interactive music creation setup using a pretrained music transformer, enabling real-time jamming without the need for fine-tuning. The setup involves a MIDI keyboard and the Anticipatory Music Transformer, which plays a second piano. The transformer adjusts its output based on the user&\#x27;s input, including key, chord, and tempo, creating a dynamic and responsive music creation experience.

**「Anticipatory Music Transformer Background」** The Anticipatory Music Transformer is a type of music transformer that has been developed to facilitate co-creation of music. This model is designed to work with a temporal point process, which is a type of event process, and is conditioned asynchronously on realizations of a second, correlated process known as the control process. The transformer is capable of performing infilling control tasks, including accompaniment, which allows for a composer to have fine-grained control over the collaboration between human and AI in music creation. The models are trained using the large and diverse Lakh MIDI music dataset and are reported to match the performance of autoregressive models for prompted music generation. They have been evaluated by human experts who found that the accompaniments produced by the anticipatory model have musicality similar to that of music composed by humans.

**「Consequence for Users」** The interactive music creation setup using a pretrained music transformer allows users to engage in real-time jamming without the need for fine-tuning. This approach enhances the accessibility of music creation for individuals who may not have the resources or expertise to train complex models, thereby broadening the participation in music technology.
