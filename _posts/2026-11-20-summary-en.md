**「Background on Token Fraud in AI Platforms」** Token fraud in AI platforms, such as ChatPRD, has become a significant issue. ChatPRD is an AI platform designed to assist product managers in transforming ideas into specifications. It has been operational since the GPT-4 era and has been developed publicly for much of that time. The platform offers a free trial, which has been exploited by groups for token fraud. **「Impact on AI Platforms」** The rise of token fraud in AI platforms, such as ChatPRD, has led to significant misuse of resources. This not only affects the legitimate users of these platforms but also poses a threat to the sustainability and integrity of AI services. The unauthorized use of AI platforms for commercial purposes, as seen in the example of token scammers using free trials to build Roblox and Fortnite maps, highlights the need for robust security measures to protect against such fraudulent activities.
