<div class="story-actions"><a class="story-action story-action--primary" href="https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/17/external-sandboxes-for-coding-agents/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Official source</a><button class="copy-story-link" type="button" data-copy-url="https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/17/external-sandboxes-for-coding-agents/">Copy link</button></div>

**「Background on External Sandboxing for Coding Agents」** External sandboxes for coding agents are a novel approach to enhancing safety in executing local models. This concept involves running code executed by agents inside a dedicated sandbox, while the agent itself remains outside of that sandbox. This method is particularly relevant in software engineering and AI systems, as it allows for the safe experimentation with different approaches to running untrusted code. Prime Radiant, an AI research lab known for its work on Superpowers and other projects, has been involved in this research. Prime Radiant emphasizes the importance of building tools that work for people rather than the other way round, reflecting the lab&\#x27;s focus on human-centric AI development. The concept of sandboxing AI-generated code execution has gained attention in recent years, with various approaches and tools being developed to ensure security and control over the execution environment.

**「Impact on Software Engineering and AI Systems」** The introduction of external sandboxes for coding agents, as developed by Prime Radiant, enhances the safety of executing local models by isolating the code execution within a dedicated sandbox. This approach mitigates the risk of damaging actions on a machine due to mistakes or prompt injection attacks, thereby improving the security and reliability of AI systems and software engineering practices.
