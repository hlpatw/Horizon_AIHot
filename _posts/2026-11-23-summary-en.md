### Interactive AI Game Experience ⭐️ 7.0/10

A game has been developed that allows users to experience being a subagent of an LLM, generating some community interest and discussion. The game is an interesting approach to interactive learning and engagement with AI, though it is not a groundbreaking development in the field.

hackernews · ivantgam · Nov 22, 13:56 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50599762)

<div class="story-actions"><a class="story-action story-action--primary" href="https://github.com/gulivan/worker755" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Community discussion</a><button class="copy-story-link" type="button" data-copy-url="https://github.com/gulivan/worker755">Copy link</button><span class="story-access-note">Some community sites may be unavailable on certain networks.</span></div>

**「Background on AI Interaction and Middle Management」** The concept of &\#x27;reverse centaurs,&\#x27; as termed by Cory Doctorow, refers to machines using humans as assistants, a metaphor that has gained relevance in discussions about AI and automation. This idea is explored in various contexts, such as in automation theory where a reverse centaur is described as a machine that uses a human being as its assistant. Additionally, there is a growing trend in the use of AI to eliminate middle management roles, with predictions that by 2026, 20% of organizations will leverage AI to eliminate more than half of their current middle management positions, reshaping organizational hierarchies.

**「Impact on Users and Developers」** The game serves as an educational tool for users to understand the concept of AI subagents and the potential roles they could play in managing AI services. For developers, it provides a novel approach to interactive learning and engagement with AI, potentially inspiring new ideas and applications in the field of AI interaction and software engineering.

**「Community Discussion」** Users have expressed varying opinions on the game. Some find it fun and engaging, while others point out limitations such as the game manager always providing exact commands, suggesting a lack of autonomy in the gameplay. There is also a discussion about the potential reality of humans working for AI, with some users speculating that this kind of middle manager role for AI could become a reality soon.
