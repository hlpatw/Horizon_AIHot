### The New York Times Warns Photographers Not to Update Photoshop Over AI Features ⭐️ 7.0/10

<div class="story-actions"><a class="story-action story-action--primary" href="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1041227/the-new-york-times-warns-photographers-not-to-update-photoshop-over-ai-features" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Community discussion</a><button class="copy-story-link" type="button" data-copy-url="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1041227/the-new-york-times-warns-photographers-not-to-update-photoshop-over-ai-features">Copy link</button><span class="story-access-note">Some community sites may be unavailable on certain networks.</span></div>

**「Background on Photoshop&\#x27;s AI Features」** Adobe Photoshop has recently introduced generative AI features such as Harmonize, Generative Upscale, Generate Image, and Generative Fill. These features utilize AI to enhance and modify images, offering tools that can upscale images, create new content based on descriptions, and fill in areas of an image automatically. The introduction of these AI features has sparked a debate about the impact of AI on the credibility of photographers and the potential stigma associated with AI-generated content.

**「Impact on Photographers&\#x27; Credibility」** Photographers are concerned about the potential stigma associated with AI-generated content, which could negatively impact their credibility, especially on social media platforms where an &\#x27;AI generated content&\#x27; tag could be detrimental to their reputation.

**「Community Discussion」** Photographers and industry professionals are expressing concerns about the difficulty in distinguishing between AI-generated and manually edited images. There is a fear that being associated with AI-generated content could negatively impact credibility and brand reputation. Some are also suggesting that the move to subscription-free software models could be an opportunity for competitors to gain market share.
